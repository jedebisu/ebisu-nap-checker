## What This Looks Like on Device

The collapsed NAP detail bar now sits **12px higher** off the screen bottom, providing:

1. **More breathing room** between the bar and the Android navigation/gesture area
2. **Clearer separation** — users won't accidentally trigger system gestures when tapping the bar
3. **Better visual hierarchy** — the bar appears to "float" more distinctly above the map surface
4. **Preserved usability** — still easily tappable; click target is still large (full-width with 72px height)
